Key ideas

🪙 Coins, not balances. Your balance is the sum of coins you can spend.
✂ Spend whole coins. Anything left over comes back as change.
🔥 Spent coins are consumed. Reusing one is rejected by every node.
⛏ Leftover inputs are the fee that rewards the miner.
Every coin spends once.
