Why vaults hold up

🏦 Overcollateralized. Every stablecoin is backed by more value than it’s worth.
📉 Liquidation. Below the minimum ratio, collateral is sold to repay the debt.
🔨 Auctions turn collateral into stablecoins to cover the loss.
⚠ Risks: violent crashes, congested chains and bad oracles.
