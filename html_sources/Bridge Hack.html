What to learn

🔑 Few keys, big risk. A small guardian set is a single point of failure.
🧾 Verify on-chain. Prefer cryptographic proofs over trusting signers.
🪙 Wrapped means borrowed trust. A token is only as safe as its bridge.
🚦 Limit blast radius. Rate limits, monitoring, audits.
