Why it’s trust-minimized

🔭 Light clients verify the other chain’s headers and proofs.
🚚 Relayers are untrusted couriers. They can’t forge a valid proof.
🔒 Lock and mint. Tokens lock on the source and appear as vouchers on the destination.
🧱 Security comes from the chains’ own validators, not a third-party bridge.
